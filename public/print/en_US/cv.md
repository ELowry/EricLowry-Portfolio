## Professional Summary

I am a developer, systems designer, coach, and game designer.  
I hold a master's in architecture with an extra specialization in landscape design, which ignited my interest in crafting human experiences and environments. Combining this with a passion for digital spaces and video games, I pivoted and spent a decade building a video game for business skills development, coaching leaders, and working on sales and marketing.

My training as a coach has focused on teamwork skills, distributed leadership, conflict resolution, and crisis management. I have helped numerous teams and leaders transition from traditional siloed hierarchical structures to a more organic skills-driven "team-of-teams" methodology. This has proven useful in helping teams face the onslaught of new technologies that transform workflows and production pipelines.

Ultimately, my greatest asset lies in the planning and execution of creative and user-focused digital projects. Whether working on video games, websites, or even sales-focused content, I care deeply about creating accessible experiences that align with actual human behavior, needs, and interests. This includes helping my teammates by creating tooling and workflows that help accelerate their own work and reduce the cognitive load of repetitive or cumbersome tasks.

I am a strong advocate for open-source development, actively building tools and contributing back to the ecosystem that supports my own projects.
