# Privacy Policy

## What I Store

This website is built on [Google's Firebase platform](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and uses _Firebase Hosting_. As a result, your **IP address** will be stored by the platform for a few months to avoid misuse of the platform.

## Cookies & Storage

No tracking cookies are used on this site.

## Data Rights

Your access and deletion rights for data processed by my external service providers (Firebase/Google) can be handled directly through their respective privacy portals.
