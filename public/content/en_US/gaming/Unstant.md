# Unstant

![Artistically rendered view of a yellow desert with a red buoy](/assets/images/gaming/unstant/header__240-135-webp_240-135_400-225-webp_400-225_600-337-webp_600-337_820-461-webp_820-461_1400-787-webp_1400-787_1875-1054-webp_1875-1054.jpg)

As a student of architecture, I've always been fascinated the work of designing spaces for and about human experience and use.

Following a masters thesis about what video games bring to the table for architecture and architects, a fellow student and I made a full video game from the ground up as our final Masters degree project:

[Watch The Trailer](https://spectra.video/videos/embed/dk7DvpkYYMWMMrbJNgo1f8?aspect=56.25%)

Unstant, a video game built in Unreal Engine, was the result of wanting to experiment with a new design methodology that adapted architectural practices to the virtual world.
