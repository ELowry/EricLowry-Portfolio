### Context

Beyond the operational and technical development of Disruptive Learning Solutions (DLS), I have served as the primary force behind our public-facing media and communications for nearly a decade. This has encompassed everything from designing our foundational brand identity to directing video podcasts and managing a multilingual editorial pipeline.

My goal has always been to ensure that our messaging remains clear, professional, and engaging, translating complex pedagogical and technological concepts into accessible content targeted toward B2B executives and L&D professionals.

### Brand Identity & Art Direction

I designed most logos, and established the core brand identities for DLS, our flagship product CinQ, and various projects that we have established with partners.

#### Video Podcasts

I directed and edited a comprehensive 30+ episode video podcast series in English, followed by a shorter French spin-off.

These were recorded entirely remotely using a flexible mix of Google Meet, Discord, and local OBS capture to balance participant ease-of-use with post-production fidelity. Over time the planning, recording, editing, and publishing processes were optimized to a roughly half-day procedure for final distribution across YouTube and most common podcasting platforms.

### Editorial Workflow & Knowledge Vault

#### Multilingual Publishing

Managing the English/French translation and localization of all DLS sales, marketing, and social media copy; ensuring that the tone and technical terminology remained consistent and relevant across cultural lines.

#### Rigorous Editing

I acted as the primary gatekeeper for readability, grammar, and spelling for DLS copy and articles. This was made easier using tools such as LanguageTool and my own custom plugin, [Obsidian Replace Commands](/content/en_US/osd/obsidian-replace-commands.md), to clean up recurring author quirks and typos automatically.

I was also in charge of fact-checking quotes, statistics, and external references for all our published articles, videos, and social media posts.

#### Visual Curation

Each published article was illustrated and visually enhanced using a mix of our own photography, copyright-free media, and curated AI-generated illustrations.

> [!NOTE]
> While we briefly experimented with generative AI for visual media, the team rapidly decided to stop generating new images due to ethical considerations regarding the unauthorized use of human artists' work in training models.

#### Knowledge Architecture

Finally, to ensure that this massive volume of writing retained its long-term value, I architected a dedicated shared Obsidian vault containing every published article. Each entry was tagged and connected using the "Smart Connections" plugin to group content methodically by topic, tone, and subject. This was done with the objective of eventually collating these articles into published books.
