# Coaching, Business & Operations

My path into business operations and executive coaching is not the result of attending business school or following dedicated courses.  
Instead, I was trained by colleagues and business partners throughout my work at Disruptive Learning Solutions (DLS).

My background studying Architecture had already required that I focus on self-managed project planning and execution, both individually and within teams, which made it easy to transition into organizing a start-up.

Being a managing partner in a small and agile company means having responsibilities across the entire operational spectrum. Over the course of a decade, I co-developed the DLS business and the CinQ brand, navigating everything from brand strategy and technical project management to pre-sales engineering and B2B marketing.

Simultaneously, a colleague with over a decade of high-level executive coaching initiated my training in behavioral diagnostics and executive facilitation. While I do not hold formal coaching certifications, my expertise was forged through a decade of hands-on mentorship from veteran corporate executives and leadership experts.  
I have since personally coached hundreds of teams across a massive variety of cultures and roles; ranging from top-tier professional esports teams in the Netherlands to Fortune 500 business leaders from companies such as SBM, Stellantis, Sanofi, Lacoste, Auchan, and BNP Paribas.

This hands-on, multidisciplinary journey has given me a rich, practical understanding of how to run a team, scale a product, and drive measurable behavioral change in human teams.
