# Architectural & Landscape Design Projects

The following is a curated selection of architecture and landscape design projects from my studies at ENSA-V and ENSP-V:

- **[BASELAND](/content/en_US/architecture/projects/BASELAND.md)**  
  Automating the visual identity and asset workflow for a massive, collaborative digital landscape model.
- **[Eco-Friendly Student Housing](/content/en_US/architecture/projects/student-housing.md)**  
  Designing an ecological, community-focused wetland housing concept for the 2014 Solar Decathlon.
- **["La Mariée Japonaise" Pavilion](/content/en_US/architecture/projects/pavilion.md)**  
  A shaded art installation and resting area taken from complex 3D digital modeling to physical fabrication.
- **[Plateau de Saclay Train Station](/content/en_US/architecture/projects/station.md)**  
  Conceptualizing an urban transit hub designed to dismantle physical barriers and act as the foundational "seed" for a new city.
